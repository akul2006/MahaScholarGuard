## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a high-integrity, institutional yet empathetic digital environment tailored for Maharashtra students, parents, and first-generation applicants navigating complex scholarship documentation.

### Brand Personality & Emotional Tenor
- **Authoritative & Reliable:** Evokes institutional credibility, precision, and diligence without the archaic bureaucracy or friction of standard legacy portals.
- **Supportive & Guiding:** Reassuring, structured, and crystal-clear. It reduces cognitive overwhelm for students who cannot afford filing mistakes.
- **Transparent & Independent:** Distinctly demarcates verified readiness criteria from direct governmental authority through clear, pristine status cues.

### Aesthetic Direction: Modern Civic Clarity
The aesthetic is a calibrated hybrid of **Modern Corporate** reliability and **Tactile Utility**:
- **Structured Cadence:** High data density balanced by generous optical breathing room.
- **Crisp Geometry:** High-contrast typographic scale paired with razor-sharp structural card layouts.
- **Functional Semantics:** Purpose-driven chromatic signaling—every color informs document verification, warnings, or missing certificates with absolute lucidity.

## Colors

The palette leverages authoritative deep navy tones balanced by vibrant status semantics, grounded upon a clean, low-strain background.

### Hierarchy & Functional Assignment
- **Primary Navy (`#0F1E36`):** The foundation of trust. Anchors the primary headers, navigational structures, major brand landmarks, and primary actionable states.
- **Secondary Royal Blue (`#1E40AF`):** Drives secondary interactions, navigational links, active selection rings, and progress tracks.
- **Tertiary Teal / Emerald (`#0D9488`):** Reserved strictly for successful outcomes: "Verified", "Ready for Submission", "Seal Intact", and official readiness indicators.
- **Neutral Foundation Canvas (`#F8FAFC`):** A soft, eye-protective tinted base that eliminates harsh white glare while making card containers pop.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF`):** Pure white reserved exclusively for interactive and static content containers.
- **Structural Outlines (`#E2E8F0`):** Precise bounding lines that delineate sections and prevent visual bleed.

### Semantic Intent & Status Palette
- **Verified / Document Valid:** `#0D9488` (Teal) with subtle tint `#F0FDFA` for pill backgrounds.
- **Warning / Review Needed:** `#D97706` (Warm Ochre) for minor discrepancies, impending expiration, or non-critical corrections.
- **Missing / Action Required:** `#DC2626` (Crimson Soft) strictly reserved for missing documents, invalid stamps, or blocker items.

## Typography

The typography leverages **Inter** across all layers, calibrated specifically for legible data verification, form fields, and multilingual bilingual guidance (English/Marathi).

### Reading Hierarchy & Contrast Rules
- **Headers:** Rendered in `headline-xl` through `headline-sm` with tight tracking (`-0.02em` to `-0.01em`) in Primary Navy (`#0F1E36`) to produce firm visual anchors.
- **Body & Guidance Text:** Set with relaxed line heights (`1.5` to `1.6`) to provide unhurried scanning of lengthy eligibility guidelines.
- **Labels & Status Pills:** High-contrast, medium-to-bold weights with slight letter tracking (`0.01em` to `0.03em`) to distinguish metadata from operational inputs.

## Layout & Spacing

A disciplined 12-column grid system designed to scale gracefully from budget mobile devices (frequent among students) up to desktop verification workstations.

### Grid & Breakpoints
- **Desktop (1024px+):** 12-column fluid grid, `2rem` outer margins, `1.5rem` gutters. Maximum content canvas constrained to `1280px` for optimal reading ergonomics.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px):** 8-column grid, `1.5rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column grid, `1rem` outer margins, `1rem` gutters. Cards and checklist matrices shift into vertical, linear cards.

### Spacing Distribution
- `space-xs` (4px) and `space-sm` (8px): Used for internal pill margins, icon-to-label offsets, and inline status badges.
- `space-md` (16px): Standard gap for vertical form elements, document list stacks, and internal card padding on mobile.
- `space-lg` (24px): Standard desktop container padding and inter-card distances.
- `space-xl` (40px): Section dividers and module headers.

## Elevation & Depth

To preserve an authentic, clear civic interface, this system rejects intense blur filters and heavy drop shadows in favor of crisp delineation through borders and gentle ambient depth.

### Depth Hierarchy
- **Level 0 (Canvas Base):** Background canvas in `#F8FAFC`. Zero elevation.
- **Level 1 (Default Containers & Cards):** Pure white surface (`#FFFFFF`) framed with a `1px` solid outline in `#E2E8F0`. Elevation supported by a restrained ambient drop: `box-shadow: 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.05), 0 1px 2px -1px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.05)`.
- **Level 2 (Active Document Cards & Hover States):** Sustained `1px` border (shifting to `#CBD5E1` on interaction) lifted with: `box-shadow: 0 4px 6px -1px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.07), 0 2px 4px -2px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.05)`.
- **Level 3 (Modal Alerts & Document Preview Drawers):** High-priority overlays: `box-shadow: 0 10px 15px -3px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.1), 0 4px 6px -4px rgba(15, 30, 54, 0.05)`.
- **Level 4 (Non-Governmental Disclaimer Banners):** Floating or inline alert boxes use zero shadow, a `1px` border in amber or slate, and a gentle tinted fill to maintain an objective tone.

## Shapes

The design system employs a **Rounded** shape language (`roundedness: 2`). This provides a balance between formal structure and approachable ergonomics.

### Corner Radius Standards
- **Interactive Controls (Buttons, Inputs):** `0.5rem` (8px). Delivers a modern, click-accessible structure without childlike over-rounding.
- **Cards, Panels, and Containers:** `rounded-lg` (`1rem` / 16px). Softens large content sections while maintaining rectangular discipline.
- **Modals & Drawers:** `rounded-xl` (`1.5rem` / 24px) for desktop containers, or pinned flat on bottom sheets for mobile.
- **Status Tags, Badges & Pills:** Full pill format (`9999px`) to create an immediate visual distinction between status metadata and actionable square buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Verify Document", "Run Readiness Check"):** Solid `#0F1E36` fill, `#FFFFFF` label text, `0.5rem` radius. Hover state transitions to `#1E40AF`.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Upload Alternative", "Save Draft"):** Transparent fill with a `1.5px` border in `#1E40AF` and text in `#1E40AF`.
- **Tertiary / Utility:** Flat transparent button with subtle `#0F1E36` text; turns `#F1F5F9` on hover.

### Document Status Badges & Pills
- Built with a `9999px` border radius, high-legibility `label-sm` or `label-md` weight, and an inline status indicator dot or icon.
- **Verified / Ready:** Emerald surface (`#F0FDFA`), emerald border (`#CCFBF1`), deep teal text (`#0F766E`).
- **Needs Review:** Warm Ochre surface (`#FFFBEB`), amber border (`#FDE68A`), dark amber text (`#92400E`).
- **Missing / Action Required:** Crimson Soft surface (`#FEF2F2`), soft crimson border (`#FECACA`), dark red text (`#991B1B`).

### Document Checklist Cards
- Background: `#FFFFFF`.
- Perimeter: `1px` solid `#E2E8F0`.
- Internal Layout: Left indicator showing status icon, title with Marathi/English bilingual subtext, file upload metadata, and a right-aligned action trigger button.
- Focus/Reviewing State: Left border thickness upgrades to `4px` solid in the respective state color (`#0D9488`, `#D97706`, or `#DC2626`).

### Input Fields & Upload Drops
- **Input Fields:** Pure white background, `1px` border in `#CBD5E1`, internal padding `space-sm` to `space-md`, text in `#0F1E36`. Active focus state generates a `2px` ring in `#1E40AF` with zero glow.
- **Upload Dropzones:** Subtle dashed line (`2px`) in `#94A3B8` over `#F8FAFC`, transitioning to `#1E40AF` dashed border with `#EFF6FF` background when a document is dragged over.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Crisp `0.25rem` radius on checkboxes, full circular radius on radio buttons.
- Border in `#94A3B8`, switching to a solid `#1E40AF` fill with white checkmark when checked.

### Non-Governmental Disclaimer Ribbon
- High-prominence, muted informational banner positioned atop key flows.
- Styled with a `1px` solid border in `#CBD5E1`, neutral slate fill (`#F1F5F9`), displaying an informational shield icon and unequivocal microcopy: *"Independent Scholarship Assistant. Not an official agency of MahaDBT or the Government of Maharashtra."*
